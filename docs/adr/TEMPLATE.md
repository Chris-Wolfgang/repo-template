## Context

What problem, constraint or pressure makes this decision necessary? Describe the situation as it is: the technical
facts, the options on the table and what pulls in different directions. Someone reading this in a year should
understand why the question came up without digging through PRs.

## Decision

The choice, in one or two sentences of active voice ("We will ..."). Then the reasoning: why this option beat the
others.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A.** Why it was rejected.
- **Option B.** Why it was rejected.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what this commits future changes to. Include the downsides; a record
that lists only upsides is not a record. Note any follow-up work.
